# Licenses and attribution

The MIT license at the repository root covers the original FIM Workbench implementation, launcher, packaging and verification scripts, study fixture and Workbench documentation. An upstream model carries its contributor's terms into a generated working copy. The source checkout contains research models, weights, support files and derived artifacts under `verification/`. Review the corresponding row before reusing those materials. [Workbench MIT license](LICENSE) · [Pinned revisions and file checksums](benchmarks/source-lock.json)

| Material | Origin and terms |
|---|---|
| FIM fault catalogue | Ezio Bartocci, Leonardo Mariani, Dejan Ničković and Drishti Yadav described the fault operators. Workbench implements them independently. The optional comparison study uses the participant's separate clone of the authors' implementation, MIT, copyright © 2022 Drishti Yadav. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3540250.3558932) · [Pinned FIM license](https://gitlab.com/DrishtiYadav/fimtool/-/blob/a110acf2b718eab4cdf65f938203f60009fa0510/LICENSE) |
| ARCH-COMP Falsification collection | The repository leaves ownership and licensing with each contributor and grants ARCH-COMP and its organizers permission for non-commercial purposes including reproducing competition results. For third-party redistribution, consult the individual contributor terms. [Collection license](https://gitlab.com/goranf/ARCH-COMP/-/blob/2238327987e2e3b81f9bcc38b2ad250013deba04/LICENSE) · [Falsification collection](https://gitlab.com/goranf/ARCH-COMP/-/tree/2238327987e2e3b81f9bcc38b2ad250013deba04/models/FALS) |
| AT and NN | The competition records identify the Breach model revisions. NN also cites the MathWorks NARMA-L2 example. AT uses MathWorks demo data at `verification/sources/at_data/sldemo_autotrans_data.mat`. A separate redistribution grant for those demo assets has not been established here. [AT provenance](verification/sources/ARCH/transmission/requirements.txt) · [NN provenance](verification/sources/ARCH/neural/requirements.txt) |
| AFC, CC and SC | The respective competition folders identify the models and publications. The pinned folders provide attribution and benchmark requirements. We have not identified a general redistribution grant in those folders. [AFC](verification/sources/ARCH/powertrain/requirements.txt) · [CC](verification/sources/ARCH/chasing-cars/requirements.txt) · [SC](verification/sources/ARCH/steam-condenser/requirements.txt) |
| F16 and R2026a compatibility files | The AeroBenchVV tree includes GPLv3 and separate notices for components such as flypath3d. The three compatibility files under `verification/sources/compat_f16` come from the Falsify project, whose root includes GPLv2. Two adapt AeroBench routines. Preserve both upstream notices and consult the relevant file and component terms when redistributing an adaptation. [AeroBench license](verification/sources/ARCH/f16-gcas/AeroBenchVV-develop/LICENSE.txt) · [flypath3d credits](verification/sources/ARCH/f16-gcas/AeroBenchVV-develop/src/main/viewers/flypath3d/readme_AeroBenchVV.txt) · [Falsify](https://github.com/31916/Falsify) |

| SB1–SB5 | The FalBenchGen authors supply five models, trained weights and scripts. We did not find an explicit license in the pinned repository. Seek permission from the rights holders before redistributing these assets or derived copies. [Pinned FalBenchGen repository](https://github.com/lyudeyun/FalBenchGen_ARCHCOMP25/tree/4d4729f428780a8a01bc0173873506c8d71a9f38) |
| MATLAB and Simulink | The software required to run Workbench and model-specific toolboxes is licensed separately by MathWorks. [MathWorks licensing](https://www.mathworks.com/company/aboutus/policies_statements.html) |

The 2.2 installer ships Workbench and the MIT-licensed PM example. Users who have the research model data can connect the extracted source checkout once in the launcher. The full checkout and older releases also contain the upstream materials listed above. [Installation and benchmark setup](README.md#install-and-run)
